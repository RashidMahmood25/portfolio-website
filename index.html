About

Founder, builder, and everyday learner.

I'm Rashid Mahmood, founder of Rust Wheel — a car and bike marketplace for Pakistan. I started it because buying and selling a car shouldn't cost a heavy commission or a pile of confusing paperwork.

Alongside the business I build small, useful tools in public on GitHub — calculators, converters, trackers — and contribute to open-source projects, so the work (and the learning) stays visible.
